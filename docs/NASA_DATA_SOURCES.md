# NASA, partner and research sources

## NASA resources used as design references

| Resource | Connection to the prototype |
| --- | --- |
| [Space Communications: 7 Things You Need to Know](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/tech-demonstration/space-communications-7-things-you-need-to-know/) | Approximately 4–24 minutes of one-way Mars latency informs Silent Hour. Any added ground-review time is a prototype assumption, not a NASA requirement. |
| [Risk from Inadequate Sleep and Irregular Schedules](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-from-inadequate-sleep-and-irregular-schedules/) | Sleep/workload support and routine planning; does not validate application thresholds. |
| [Hazard: Isolation and Confinement](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/) | Isolation, stress and social-support context. |
| [CONNECT During Social Isolation](https://www.nasa.gov/podcasts/houston-we-have-a-podcast/connect-during-social-isolation/) | Supportive routine and social-connection prompts, not therapy. |
| [Human Research Program risks](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/) | Wider spaceflight-health risk context. |

**The repository does not import NASA astronaut-level health records. All mission measurements are synthetic. Referencing NASA publications does not imply endorsement.**

## Partner technology reference

[Canadian Space Agency Bio-Monitor](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/bio-monitor.asp) informs a future wearable-integration concept. No CSA hardware, software or data feed is connected.

## Other scientific and medical references

- [IFRC first-aid guidelines](https://www.ifrc.org/document/international-first-aid-resuscitation-and-education-guidelines): the inherited knowledge base names the 2020 edition. A later edition exists. Terrestrial material is not approved spacecraft guidance.
- [Royal College of Physicians NEWS2](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/improving-care/resources/national-early-warning-score-news-2/): selected physiological bands, not full certified NEWS2 implementation. No official scoring chart is added to this package.
- [Basner, Mollicone and Dinges (2011), PVT-B](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3197786/): research basis for the attention-task concept, not validation of this custom task.
- [Barger et al. (2014), astronaut sleep deficiency](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4188436/): background context, not imported participant data.

The inherited project also names Van Dongen (2003), Jones (2022), NASA/NSBRI sex-and-gender material (2014), and US Office on Women's Health heart-attack material. Exact claim mapping, source URLs where missing, quotation permissions and coverage must be reconciled with the team's separate technical documentation before publication. Their inclusion in inherited JSON is not proof of clinical validity or redistribution rights.
